Non-Teaching Hours and Teacher Stress in Singapore: Non-Linear Evidence from TALIS 2024

Using OECD TALIS 2024 data, this study evaluates how ten non-teaching tasks impact Singapore teacher stress across linear, non-linear, and robustness models. Marking and administrative duties increase stress at a declining rate past specific hour thresholds, while CCA hours show no non-linear effect, and teamwork hours and collaborative school culture significantly buffer workload strain.

Household Food Spending and Taxation: Evidence from State and Year Fixed Effects, 2020–2024

Using United States Department of Agriculture food sales data and Current Population Survey demographic records from 2020 to 2024, this study examines how grocery and restaurant sales tax rates affect household food spending across U.S. states using two-way fixed effects models. It finds that grocery taxes significantly increase household food spending with lower-income households showing about twice the sensitivity of higher-income households whereas restaurant taxes have no significant impact on food spending for either group.

Customer Satisfaction and Revisit Intention: Evidence from Koufu and SMU

This study examines customer satisfaction and repeat visits at Koufu SMU using a survey of 159 customers. The findings show that convenient location brings customers in, while price and food quality keep them coming back. Additionally, satisfaction drives word-of-mouth recommendations, and older customers value comfort more than younger ones.

Causal Effect of Resource Sustainability Act on Targeted Waste Categories in Singapore

This study evaluates Singapore's Resource Sustainability Act (RSA) from 2000 to 2024 using difference-in-differences and synthetic control methods to measure its effect on recycling rates. The findings reveal a striking contrast across waste streams: packaging recycling dropped by 38% due to short-term compliance disruptions, metals showed no measurable change because recycling rates were already near 100%, while food waste demonstrated an early rise in recycling performance following the mandate.
